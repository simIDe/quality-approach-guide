# Product sheet: Weekly training load, running club

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Product name** | Weekly training load |
| **User** | The coach of the running club |
| **Decision** | Every Monday, lighten, keep or increase each athlete's load for the week |
| **Owner** | The group that maintains the example (here, the teacher) |

## Promise

- **Content:** one row per athlete per week, with the number of sessions, the minutes run and the load (perceived exertion from 0 to 10 × duration in minutes, in arbitrary units).
- **Timeliness:** the eight weeks ending on the end date of the data (Sunday 22 November 2026). The timeliness check fails if the latest session is more than seven days before that date.
- **Scope:** the six athletes in the sessions file, over eight weeks. Sessions are checked along five dimensions (accuracy, completeness, timeliness, consistency, uniqueness) and every defect found is written in the report with its handling.

## What is not guaranteed

- Perceived exertion is reported by the athlete: it stays subjective.
- A week with no recorded session is flagged, not filled in: it does not appear in the table.
- A session whose exertion is out of scale is excluded: the load of that week is underestimated.
- A duration under ten minutes is assumed to have been entered in hours and is converted. A real session shorter than ten minutes would therefore be wrongly corrected.
- The product does not say which load is right for an athlete: the decision stays with the coach.

## Data used and origin

| Source | Origin | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data/sessions.csv`: one row per session (athlete, date, duration, perceived exertion) | Generated by `generate.py`, fixed seed, four defects introduced on purpose | Sessions from 28 September to 22 November 2026, recreated on every run |

## Personal data

No. The data is synthetic and the athlete identifiers (A01 to A06) do not match anyone. A real club would handle health data (exertion, load): it would then have to go through the questions of the "Personal data" sheet before starting.
