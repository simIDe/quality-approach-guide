# The brief

> You deliver a data product to a specific user, as a small team would for a client. The product must stay very simple: one source, one transformation, one output are enough. You are assessed on the method more than on the result.

- **Single deadline:** delivery on Monday 18 January 2027 (product, repository, method log). Oral presentation on Wednesday 20 January.
- **Rules:** AI is allowed, agents included; every member must be able to explain any part of the project; no secret or personal data is sent to a model; a group that uses personal data, including its own, says so in its product sheet and explains how it protects it.
- **Project size:** the group alone decides, and this is an assessment criterion.

## Reducing the scope: three examples

| Too broad | Reduced version |
| --- | --- |
| A platform analysing all the training data of a club, across all sports | The weekly load of the athletes of a single training group, over the last eight weeks |
| An interactive map of all sports facilities in France, with accessibility indicators | A table of the facilities of a single town, by facility type, for that town's sports department |
| A tool that predicts the results of all the competitions of a federation | An automatic report of a club's results at one competition, produced from a single results file |

The reduced version keeps one user, one decision and one source. It can grow later if time allows.
